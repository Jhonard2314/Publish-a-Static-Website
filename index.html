About This Blog

Hi! I'm a hobby astronomer who likes pointing cheap telescopes at the night sky. This site is where I share what I've learned, what I'm looking forward to, and what's in my equipment bag.

I observe from my backyard, about 40 minutes outside the city, which is just far enough to see the planets and most meteor showers clearly.

My Stargazing Gear

Here is what I use most often when observing:

  • 150mm Dobsonian telescope
  • 8x42 binoculars
  • Red flashlight (keeps night vision)
  • Star chart / planisphere
